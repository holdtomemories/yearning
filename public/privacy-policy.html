Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Yearning v1.3.0

Yearning is built on a single belief: your inner world belongs to you alone. Your memories, thoughts, and feelings never leave your device — and they never will.

01

What we collect

Almost nothing. Yearning stores your memories — titles, body text, mood, coordinates, timestamps, and place names — exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data lives only on the physical device you're using. It is never transmitted to any server we operate.

No account. No login. No sync. There is no backend database, no user profile, and no server-side storage associated with Yearning. We could not read your memories even if we wanted to.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any identity information
  • The content of your memories or journal entries
  • Your precise or approximate GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprints
  • Crash logs that contain personal information
02

Location access

Yearning requests your device's location only when you explicitly tap Locate Me or Plant Here. Location is used solely to center the map or set the coordinates of a new memory — it is never stored on a server, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Optional location reminders (a feature you must explicitly enable) use your device's background location to quietly notify you when you're near a memory you've planted. This check happens entirely on-device; coordinates are never sent anywhere.

You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The app continues to work fully without it.

03

Third-party services

Yearning uses a small number of third-party services to render the map and look up place names. Each is covered by its own privacy policy linked below.

Service
Purpose & policy
CARTO / OpenStreetMap
Renders the map tiles. Your IP address is sent to their servers when tiles load — this is standard for any map product.
carto.com/privacy  ·  openstreetmap.org/privacy
Nominatim (OSM)
Reverse-geocodes coordinates into city / country labels and resolves place search queries. Results are cached on-device to minimise requests. Queries include approximate coordinates but no personal information.
osmfoundation.org — privacy
Google Fonts
Serves the Playfair Display and Lora typefaces. Your IP address is sent to Google when fonts load. No cookies are set.
policies.google.com/privacy
Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights
Collects anonymous, aggregate page-view and performance data (no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal information). Used only to understand how the app performs at a macro level.
vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
No advertising networks. No data brokers. No tracking pixels. Yearning has never sold, rented, or shared personal data with any third party for commercial purposes — and it never will.
04

Children's privacy

Yearning is a general-audience application. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing specific to children to collect, store, or disclose.

05

Deleting your data

Because all your memories are stored locally on your device, you are always in complete control. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual memories using the forget this button inside any pin card
  • Clear all data by clearing your browser's site data for yearningmap.com in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device removes all associated local storage

There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of — because none was ever stored there.

06

Changes to this policy

If we ever materially change how Yearning handles data, we will update the date at the top of this page and note it in the in-app changelog. Continuing to use Yearning after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

We do not anticipate changes that would compromise the core promise: your memories stay on your device and belong to you alone.

07

Contact

Questions about this policy or how Yearning handles data? Reach us at privacy@yearningmap.com . We aim to respond within 5 business days.
